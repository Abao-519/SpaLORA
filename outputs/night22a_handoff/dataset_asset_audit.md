# Low-cost external asset audit

## GSE205055 ME13 50 µm

The official GEO SuperSeries is public and contains 22 samples. It explicitly lists `GSM6799937 ME13_50um` and `GSM6801813 ME13_50um [ATAC-Seq]`; the full raw archive is about 7.6 GB. This is a useful new physical unit, but it belongs to the same spatial epigenome–transcriptome study family as the current P22 material. Night-22A did not download it. The exact paired-spot IDs, canonical mutually exclusive annotation, K and evaluation mask are not yet closed, so it is not registered as an ARI/NMI benchmark.

## Stereo-CITE mouse thymus

The SpatialGlue paper describes four Stereo-CITE mouse-thymus sections with paired RNA and protein and interprets cortex, medulla and cortex subzones. Its official processed-data Zenodo record is a single 671,921,238-byte archive covering multiple experimental units. This is an attractive independent RNA+protein source, but the paper's biological/method clusters are not automatically an independently curated full-domain ground truth. Night-22A therefore records it as a high-priority provenance/biological validation candidate and does not manufacture a K or mask.

No large data were downloaded during the audit. The next asset action, if the algorithm gate warrants it, is a bounded archive inventory plus exact label-file provenance—not immediate model evaluation.
