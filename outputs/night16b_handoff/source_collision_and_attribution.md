# Source collision and attribution boundary

- Prototype-distance unaries, Potts/CRF spatial priors, ICM and alpha-expansion are established ideas in BayesSpace/BASS/DR.SC/SCGP and classical vision/graph-cut literature.
- Multiscale spatial summaries and feature-weighted neighbourhood graphs have clear BANKSY/PRAGA precedents.
- Optional H&E views and graph/contrastive multimodal fusion have precedents including MISO, Proust, STESH, stGCL, SpatialEx/COSIE and adjacent work.
- Split/merge proposals, minimum-size guards, PCA-quantile splits and neighbour-aware boundary moves are generic clustering/segmentation machinery, not individually claimed as novel.

Night-16B therefore makes no stand-alone novelty claim for any one component. Its defensible result is an auditable clean-room composition: all lanes share a numeric producer/operator bank, cross-modal sparse authority starts can be passed through a generic non-degenerate structural repair, optional views have exact presence-mask fallback, and public benchmark HPO is exposed rather than disguised as an automatic selector. Whether this composition is sufficiently novel for a methods paper still requires a broader 2024-2026 collision review, matched strong baselines and independent contribution evidence.
