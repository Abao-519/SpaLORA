# Night-17F Stage-A formula and ablation contract

## Frozen object

The locked Night-16H candidate partitions are filtered by the universal
`SMALLEST_SCALE_INTERNAL_EDGE` feasibility rule.  The primary bank additionally
retains only `KMEANS_RETAINED_*` and `PATH_UNIFORM__KMEANS_RETAINED_*`
candidates.  Candidate weights use only the pre-evaluation molecular,
topological and persistence evidence axes.

For each registered sparse edge, weighted and equal-weight co-clustering
posteriors are materialized before annotations are opened.  The weighted
posterior and entropy uncertainty define a finite nonnegative capacity factor.
The consumer is the unchanged Night-15F dynamic unary and alpha-expansion
solver.  Public labels never enter candidate weights, posterior, energy or move
acceptance.

## Frozen grid and controls

- global `relation_mix`: 0.25, 0.50, 0.75;
- `relation_floor=0.05`, `relation_power=1`, `uncertainty_scale=1`;
- `RELATION_DISABLED`;
- `UNIFORM_MASS_MATCHED`;
- `PERMUTED_POSTERIOR`, deterministic within each scale and base-weight stratum;
- `ANALYTIC_UNWEIGHTED_POSTERIOR`;
- `DIRECT_WEIGHTED_POSTERIOR`.

Uniform, permutation and analytic controls are independently rescaled at each
graph scale to the primary factor's base-weighted pairwise mass.  Disabled is
the inherited base energy without that matching.  The same locked Night-16H
method start is used by all arms in a lane.

## Gate

For diagnostic reporting only, the direct arm chooses maximum ARI, then NMI,
across the three locked mixes.  At that same mix it must strictly exceed the
Night-16H input and all four controls in both ARI and NMI.  Stage B requires
this result on at least two of three primary lanes.  Actual result: 0/3; Stage B
is prohibited.
