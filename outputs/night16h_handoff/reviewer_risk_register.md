# Night-16H reviewer risk register

1. **Public-benchmark development leakage.** The 89-candidate banks were created
   during prior public-benchmark development.  The fixed selector is label-free
   at runtime, but its design was informed by the four-study scout.  The report
   does not call this blind confirmation.
2. **Novelty collision.** Consensus, medoid, multi-evidence ranking and spatial
   graph constraints have substantial prior art (including SMODEL and
   SpatialESD).  Current evidence supports a method-development signal, not a
   settled novelty claim.
3. **Selector simplicity.** The fixed rule switches between two standard axes.
   Its value is cross-study behavior and a shared structural feasible set, not
   a complex new optimizer.
4. **Hard-filter attribution.** Human hippocampus benefits from excluding
   structurally invalid candidates, but the other three banks were unchanged.
   The four-lane improvement therefore is not solely a singleton repair; still,
   the filter and selector effects must remain separate in figures and text.
5. **LOSO melanoma trade-off.** Strict training-only LOSO is lower than feasible
   medoid by ARI `0.004653` and NMI `0.006734`, though it remains nondegenerate.
6. **Candidate-library dependence.** Night-16H selects from a frozen library; it
   does not improve representation learning or guarantee quality when the bank
   lacks a good solution.
7. **Known K.** All four protocols use public registered K; general K selection
   is outside this revision.
